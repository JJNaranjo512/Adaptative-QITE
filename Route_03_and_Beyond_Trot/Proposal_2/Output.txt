========================================================================================
Assumptions (ours, on top of adaptive_qite.py's and adam_qite.py's)
========================================================================================
  1. Every physical choice (H2, the 5-param ansatz, SEED_A theta_0, drho at h=1e-6, rcond 1e-6, the cond(M) cap with COEFF 2000, energy_check's gate and DE_BAND_*, GROW, SHRINK, DELTA_TAU0/MIN/MAX, MAX_RETRIES, TAU_MAX) is imported from adaptive_qite.py unchanged. Its own assumption list applies.

  2. OURS: two tolerance semantics. The plan's prediction (alpha ~ 1) is pre-registered for 'explicit Euler with local error control'; for error-per-step control (e_n <= tol) with a p = 1 method, e_n ~ C dtau^2 gives dtau ~ tol^(1/2) and the memo's own E_tot ~ C T dtau^p gives global ~ tol^(1/2). alpha ~ 1 needs error per unit step (e_n/dtau <= tol). Both are run; the plan's prediction is scored as written against the per-step arm.

  3. OURS: e_n = |dE_actual - dE_pred| / 2, the estimator G5 validated against step doubling (ratio 2.060 median over the first 40 E1 steps); the /2 makes it estimate what step doubling estimates. It is an energy-space proxy, not a theta-space error.

  4. OURS: the PI law is memo Eq. (17), dtau_{n+1} = dtau_n (tol/e_n)^k_I (e_{n-1}/e_n)^k_P, at k_I = 0.15, k_P = 0.2 (p = 1). The first step is I-only. The factor is clipped to [SHRINK, GROW] (imported, not retuned) and then to [DELTA_TAU_MIN, DELTA_TAU_MAX] exactly as adam_qite_run clips. It updates once per accepted step on the surviving trial's e_n, as the Adam controller does; since tol is now separate from the band, G4's circularity is broken without feeding it rejected trials (G4 item 2 is not needed and not taken).

  5. OURS: rejection stays energy_check's band, unchanged. There is no reject-on-e_n>tol (the noiseless form of memo Eq. 14); that alternative is untested here.

  6. OURS: the reference is the EXACT-metric ODE theta_dot = pinv(M_exact, 1e-6) Y_exact (P1's exact_metric_and_force) solved by DOP853 at rtol 1e-11, atol 1e-14, self-checked against rtol 1e-13. The fd ODE the controllers integrate cannot be solved that tightly (DOP853 at rtol 1e-8 stalls on the ~1e-10 fd noise, found while sizing the reference), so the fd-vs-exact gap is measured by an fd DOP853 solve at rtol 1e-7 and reported as a floor: errors below 10x it are shown but kept out of the fit.

  7. OURS: the primary error is |E_run(tau_end) - E_ref(tau_end)| at each run's own tau_end; the secondary is the max over accepted steps of |E_run(tau_n) - E_ref(tau_n)|. The plan's 'final E - E_floor' is printed too but is the wrong quantity for proportionality: at tau = 3 the flow itself has not converged, so it mixes truncation error with the distance the exact flow still has to go.

  8. OURS, fixed before the sweep: a run enters the alpha fit iff it exits on tau_max, the cap binds on < 25 % of its steps, Delta tau sits at DELTA_TAU_MIN/MAX on < 25 %, and its primary error is > 10x the reference floor. alpha is the least-squares log-log slope over those runs. The 10x floor criterion was added after sizing the reference and before any controller run.

  9. OURS: the 'break tol' is the first grid tol (ascending) whose local slope to the previous point is < alpha_fit/2; the 'cap tol' is the first grid tol with >= 25 % of steps cap-bound. The plan's break prediction holds iff both exist within one grid point.

  10. No Adam arm is in the sweep: the plan's precondition (the G2 fix, beta1 = beta2) is not met.
