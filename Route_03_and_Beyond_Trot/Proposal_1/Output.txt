Assumptions (ours, on top of adaptive_qite.py's and adam_qite.py's)
========================================================================================
  1. Every physical choice (H2 Hamiltonian, ansaetze, drho at h=1e-6, the Route-3 gate, the cap, the seeds, the Adam law) is imported from adaptive_qite.py / adam_qite.py unchanged. Their own assumption lists apply.

  2. OURS: the exact metric uses the state form of plan sec. 2, M_jk = 2(Re<Gt_j Gt_k> - <Gt_j><Gt_k>), Y_k = -i<[Gt_k, H]>, with Gt_k = P_k G_k P_k^dag, P_k the gates after k, G_k = P/2 for RX/RY/RZ and |1><1| (x) P/2 for CRX/CRZ. Gate matrices come from qml.matrix on adaptive_qite.gate_list, so wire order and sign conventions are PennyLane's, not re-derived. Valid for p = 0 (pure states) only; every run in this script is noiseless.

  3. OURS: the structural rank r = 6 is fixed a priori from 2d-2, never fitted to a spectrum.

  4. OURS: rank_r_cond is reported in the Frobenius form ||M||_F sqrt(sum_{i<=r} sigma_i^-2), which is what adaptive_qite.frobenius_cond returns when pinv keeps exactly r values, so the comparison with the logged cond_M is like for like. The spectral form sigma_1/sigma_r is printed alongside.

  5. OURS: 'state speed' is ||d rho/d tau||_F = sqrt(theta_dot^T M theta_dot) (exact for a pure state, since M is the Gram matrix of the d_k rho). It is the physical speed of the trajectory, independent of how the ansatz is parametrized.

  6. OURS: the regime-aware cap (target cap) is implemented by swapping adaptive_qite's global route3_indicators for one that returns rank_r_cond in the cond_M slot. The cap coefficient (2000) is untouched, as the plan required.
