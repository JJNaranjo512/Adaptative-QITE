========================================================================================
Assumptions (ours, on top of ../adaptive_qite.py's)
========================================================================================
  1. Assumptions 1-8 of ../adaptive_qite.py are inherited unchanged (finite-difference M and Y at h=1e-6 rather than Hadamard tests; no shot noise, so 'noisy' means biased-but-deterministic; the F8/M5 policy of committing a trial step once max_retries or delta_tau_min is exhausted, with accepted=False recorded; Eq. (7) debiasing applied to M and Y before the solve with the M1-corrected Y factor q^2+q(1-q)/2). This script changes the step-size law and nothing else.

  2. Reading |dE_actual - dE_pred| as an O(delta_tau^2) local truncation error proxy is OUR claim, not the memo's -- the memo never forms an error estimate for Route 3 at all (that is why R_Future_Routes.md R1 proposes step-doubling to get one). It follows from dE_pred = -(Y.theta_dot)*delta_tau being the exact first-order model of the Euler step being taken, so the leading discrepancy is the step's own second-order term. It is a proxy in the energy, not in theta: it measures the truncation error along the one direction the controller can actually observe cheaply.

  3. e_hat is normalized by the SAME band energy_check() already uses to reject a step, band = max(DE_BAND_ABS, DE_BAND_REL*|dE_pred|), so e_hat > 1 is exactly the existing reject condition. No new tolerance is introduced: the controller reuses the gate's own tolerance as its setpoint, and the gate itself is left in place unchanged.

  4. The control law delta_tau *= clip(m_e_hat**(-s_e*K_I), SHRINK, GROW) is our synthesis. The m_e_hat**(-K_I) part is Gustafsson's integral law (R3) with the tolerance folded into e_hat; the new part is the exponent being scaled by the Adam confidence ratio s_e = m_e_hat/(sqrt(v_e_hat)+EPS), which lies in (0,1] by Jensen. A consistent error signal (s_e -> 1) gets the full Gustafsson response; a noise-dominated one (s_e -> 0) drives the exponent to 0 and freezes delta_tau. GROW and SHRINK survive only as clip bounds (a trust region), no longer as the step law itself -- which is the specific thing Assumption 1 of the parent script complains about.

  5. Adam's first-moment EMA already supplies the smoothing a PI controller's I-term provides, so no separate proportional term k_P (e_{n-1}/e_n)^k_P is included in this version. That is a YAGNI call, not a claim that it would not help.

  6. The McLachlan residual channel gets its own EMA pair (m_r, v_r) and enters as a growth CEILING only: growth <- min(growth, (RESIDUAL_REL_TOL/m_r_hat)**(s_r*K_I)) when m_r_hat exceeds RESIDUAL_REL_TOL, which is <= 1 by construction. This preserves Assumption 2 of the parent script: r_n does not depend on delta_tau, so it can never justify rejecting or shrinking the CURRENT step, only withholding growth from the next one. The same K_I is reused for both channels rather than inventing a second exponent.

  7. The EMAs update once per ACCEPTED step, using the accepted trial's e_hat, and the bias correction counts accepted steps. A retry cascade therefore does not feed its rejected trials into the moments -- otherwise a single bad step would be counted several times and would poison v_e for the following ~1/(1-BETA2) steps.

  8. m_e_hat is floored at EPS before exponentiation, so an exactly-perfect prediction (dE_actual == dE_pred, which does happen at delta_tau_min) gives maximum growth rather than a division by zero. The clip to GROW makes the floor's exact value irrelevant.

  9. No constant in this file was adjusted after seeing a result. Where Adam loses to the Route-3 ratchet that is reported as the finding. The hyperparameter ablation (BETA1/BETA2 sensitivity, momentum-only vs RMS-only) is NOT in this pass; this fairness protocol stands in for it, and the ablation remains open work.
