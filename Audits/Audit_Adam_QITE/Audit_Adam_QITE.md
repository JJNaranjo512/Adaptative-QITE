# Audit — `Adam_QITE/`

Full read of `Adam_QITE/` (696-line `adam_qite.py`, 160-line README, `Output_Pre_Audit.txt`,
three figures) against its parent `Attack_Route_03/adaptive_qite.py`, which it imports. Companion
to [[QITE_Hand_Off/Audits/Audit_Project_Wide/Audit_Adaptative_QITE|Audit_Adaptative_QITE]], which covered the parent and whose 12 findings this code
inherits by construction.

Scope is **physics and code**. Vault-hygiene defects are recorded in §6 but not scheduled.

Unlike the project-wide audit, **every finding here was checked against a live run** before it was
written down. Twelve experiments (V1–V12) were run on the `qml-julia` environment; three of my
reading-stage predictions were *refuted* by them and one was half-wrong, and all four have been
rewritten as what the runs actually showed. Raw stdout for all of them is quoted in the module notes.

A fifth prediction was refuted *after* the audit was written, by the verification script that now
ships alongside it: G5's proposed $\partial_\theta M^{+}$ probe does not detect what it claimed to —
see the closing section of [[QITE_Hand_Off/Audits/Audit_Adam_QITE/G5_Error_Estimator_Validated_And_Its_Blind_Spot|G5_Error_Estimator_Validated_And_Its_Blind_Spot]]. Every number quoted
across G2, G5 and G6 whose evidence came from the lost scratchpad is regenerated by
`Adam_QITE/verify_audit.py --target all` (147 comparisons, 0 drift; stdout in
`Adam_QITE/Output_Audit_Verify.txt`), which closes the reproducibility gap this audit
itself raised as §6.2. Not everything: the script prints a *What this ledger does NOT cover*
section, and the largest gap in it is §6.3's `rcond` table — the evidence for re-attributing E3.

> [!warning] Headline
> E2's verdict — "**the premise fails**" — is not a property of the Adam controller. It is an
> artifact of the **mismatch between the two EMA horizons**: $\beta_1 = 0.9$ averages over ~10 steps
> while $\beta_2 = 0.999$ averages over ~1000, on runs of 172–500 steps. Either value works on its
> own; it is using both at once that breaks the confidence ratio $s_e$. Every *matched* pair
> completes at $\tau_{\max}$ — including $\beta_1 = \beta_2 = 0.999$ (254 steps), the very value
> blamed. Neither $\beta$ was ever varied.
> See [[QITE_Hand_Off/Audits/Audit_Adam_QITE/G2_Beta2_Horizon_Dominates_Every_Verdict|G2_Beta2_Horizon_Dominates_Every_Verdict]].
>
> E1's "a wash" verdict, by contrast, **stands, and is now better supported than the README argues**:
> Route 3 wins at matched circuit budget against all four Adam configurations, at both seeds swept.
>
> The good news is entirely unreported: the $O(\Delta\tau^2)$ "free error estimate" premise, which
> the README asserts but never tests, **is correct** — it tracks a real step-doubling estimator
> with log-correlation 0.99 at a constant factor of 2.06.
> See [[QITE_Hand_Off/Audits/Audit_Adam_QITE/G5_Error_Estimator_Validated_And_Its_Blind_Spot|G5_Error_Estimator_Validated_And_Its_Blind_Spot]].

## 1. What the code does

`adam_qite_run` (`adam_qite.py:220-320`) is `adaptive_qite_run` (`adaptive_qite.py:411-509`) with
the step law swapped and nothing else changed. Everything physical — the H$_2$ Hamiltonian, the
ansatz, finite-difference $M$ and $Y$, the Eq. (7) debiasing, the pseudoinverse solve, the
accept/reject gate, the $\mathrm{cond}(M)$ cap — is imported from the parent and never redefined.
That discipline is real and it holds: the E1 Route-3 arm reproduces Target A bit-for-bit
(172 steps, $E=-1.122993$, 2069 circuits), which is the control that makes the comparison mean
anything.

One outer step:

1. `measure_f` → $\dot\theta = M^{+}Y$, at a cost of $2N+1$ circuits (central differences).
2. `route3_indicators` → McLachlan residual, $\mathrm{cond}_F(M)$, $\sigma_{\min}$.
3. Proactive stiffness cap: $\Delta\tau \leftarrow \max(\Delta\tau_{\min},
   \min(\Delta\tau, \texttt{COEFF}/\mathrm{cond}(M), \Delta\tau_{\max}))$.
4. **Retry loop** (`:266-277`): take the trial Euler step, gate it with `energy_check`; on
   rejection $\Delta\tau \mathrel{*}= \texttt{SHRINK}$ and retry, up to `MAX_RETRIES` or
   $\Delta\tau_{\min}$.
5. Form $\hat e$, fold it into the Adam moments, commit $\theta \leftarrow \theta_{\text{trial}}$,
   $\tau \mathrel{+}= \Delta\tau$.
6. $\Delta\tau \leftarrow \mathrm{clip}(\Delta\tau \cdot \text{growth},
   \Delta\tau_{\min}, \Delta\tau_{\max})$.

## 2. What it measures

| | Scenario | Reported quantity |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Adam vs Route 3 vs fixed step, noiseless 5-param (`SEED_A`) | Steps, circuits, final $E$, energy at matched circuit budget, distance to the ansatz's variational floor |
| E2 | Adam vs Route 3 under global depolarizing noise, $p\in\{10^{-4},10^{-3},10^{-2},5\times10^{-2}\}$ (`SEED_D`) | RMS energy deviation of each noisy run from **its own** $p=0$ run |
| E3 | Rank-6-of-9 ansatz, `rcond` $\in\{10^{-8},10^{-2}\}$, four arms = controller $\times$ cap (`SEED_C`) | Final $E$, error vs exact, $\mathrm{cond}(M)$ and $\Delta\tau$ ranges, steps |

Plus `adam_signal_diagnostic`, which reports the $\hat e$ distribution and the resulting confidence
ratio — an unusually honest piece of self-instrumentation, and the thing that makes most of this
audit possible.

## 3. How $\Delta\tau$ actually gets updated

This is the question the README answers least clearly, and the answer is the root of
[[QITE_Hand_Off/Audits/Audit_Adam_QITE/G4_Controller_Cannot_Shrink|G4_Controller_Cannot_Shrink]].

$$\hat e_n = \frac{|\Delta E_{\text{actual}} - \Delta E_{\text{pred}}|}{\max(\texttt{DE\_BAND\_ABS},\ \texttt{DE\_BAND\_REL}|\Delta E_{\text{pred}}|)},\qquad
s_e = \frac{\hat m_e}{\sqrt{\hat v_e}+\varepsilon},\qquad
\text{growth} = \mathrm{clip}\big(\hat m_e^{-s_e k_I},\ \texttt{SHRINK},\ \texttt{GROW}\big)$$

with a second EMA pair on the relative McLachlan residual acting as a growth ceiling.

**The load-bearing observation: the Adam law never shrinks $\Delta\tau$. Not once, in any run.**
`Output_Pre_Audit.txt` records `growth < 1 on 0/190` steps for E1, and the mechanism is
structural, not a tuning accident. All shrinking in this controller comes from two places that are
**inherited unchanged from Route 3** — the retry loop's `SHRINK` and the $\mathrm{cond}(M)$ cap.
So $\Delta\tau$ is governed by:

| Direction | Mechanism | Whose? |
|---|---|---|
| down | retry loop, $\times\,\texttt{SHRINK}=0.5$ per rejection (`adam_qite.py:276`) | Route 3's, verbatim |
| down | proactive cap $\texttt{COEFF}/\mathrm{cond}(M)$ (`:255-262`) | Route 3's, verbatim |
| up | $\hat m_e^{-s_e k_I}$, clipped at $\texttt{GROW}=1.25$ (`:209-215`) | the new part |

The stated goal — retiring the two hand-tuned ratchet constants — is therefore **half met at
most**. `SHRINK` still runs every rejection and `GROW` still binds the growth on 10/190 E1 steps
(100 % of them in the degeneracy configuration).

## 7. What this audit did not check

- **Shot noise.** Still unmodelled (parent Assumption 7 / R5). G3's central argument is that E2
  cannot be fixed without it, but no shot-noise experiment was run here either.
- **$\beta_1$.** Only $\beta_2$ was swept. The $\beta_1$ sensitivity and the momentum-only vs
  RMS-only ablation the README lists as open work remain open.
- **Systems beyond 2-qubit H$_2$**, and seeds beyond the three already in the suite. The
  $\beta_2$ result (G2) is 3 seeds; E3's cap-off win is still $n=1$.
- The memo `Research_Papers/QITE_timestep.pdf` was not re-read for this pass; Route 3's fidelity to
  it is [[QITE_Hand_Off/Audits/Audit_Project_Wide/Audit_Adaptative_QITE|Audit_Adaptative_QITE]]'s business, not this one's.
