========================================================================================
Assumptions (ours, not the memo's)
========================================================================================
  1. The memo gives no numbers for Route 3's thresholds. RCOND_DEFAULT, DELTA_TAU0/MIN/MAX, GROW, SHRINK, STIFFNESS_CAP_COEFF, ENERGY_MONOTONE_TOL, DE_BAND_ABS/REL and RESIDUAL_REL_TOL above are all our choices, tuned only by inspection of target A's trajectory (never against a paper-reported value -- there isn't one).
  2. Combination rule for the three Route-3 indicators (our synthesis, not in the memo): cond(M) is applied as a PROACTIVE cap on delta_tau before the trial step (memo: 'cap delta_tau'); the energy-monotonicity/variance-identity check is a REACTIVE accept/reject/retry gate, since it is the only one of the three that actually depends on delta_tau (memo: 'accept the step iff ... reject/shrink on violation'); the McLachlan residual is a REACTIVE-BUT-NOT-RETRIED caution signal -- since r_n = ||M theta_dot - Y|| does not depend on delta_tau at all (it is fixed once theta_n is), retrying with a smaller step cannot change it, so a high relative residual freezes growth for the next step instead of rejecting the current one (memo: 'a spike signals ... delta_tau should shrink', read here as 'should not grow').
  3. Eq. (7) debiasing is applied to M and Y individually before the pseudoinverse solve (M <- M/(1-p)^2N, Y <- Y/(1-p)^N), matching Algorithm 1's literal step order 'measure M~, Y~; de-bias by (7); solve for k1' and the closed forms M^err=(1-p)^2N M, Y^err=(1-p)^N Y already validated in the QDP replication (Eqs. B12-B13) -- algebraically identical to the memo's own f~ = (1-p)^-N f statement when M is full rank.
  4. Eq. (9)'s literal noise floor (delta_tau_floor = (kappa_b eps_res ||theta_dot||/C_tr)^1/p) is derived for the embedded-pair estimator e_n of Routes 1/2 and is NOT computed here: Route 3 never forms e_n. Target D builds our own analogous diagnostic instead (energy-trajectory deviation from the noiseless run, debiased vs biased, vs p) and is explicitly not a reproduction of Eq. (9).
  5. M, Y are built from finite-difference density-matrix derivatives (drho, h=1e-6) on default.qubit/default.mixed, exactly the method already used and validated (against the closed form Eq. B9, to 1e-10) in the QDP replication -- not Hadamard-test circuits. The 'evaluation count' reported for target A therefore counts calls to this shared measure_f(), not physical circuit shots.
  6. Initial theta_0 is rng.uniform(-0.5, 0.5, size=N_PARAMS) at SEED=0, not the QDP replication's published Appendix-C angles -- those were chosen there to reproduce a specific reported cond(M), not as a sensible QITE starting point. rho_0 = |00>, matching the QDP replication's convention (also unstated by the source papers). Each target uses its own default_rng(SEED_{A,B,C,D}=0,1,2,3) (F7/M5), so a single target run reproduces identically whether invoked alone or as part of --target all; targets do NOT share theta_0 with each other, so any agreement between two targets' numbers is a statement about the physics, not a shared random draw.
  7. No shot noise is modelled; the global depolarizing channel is applied to the exact density matrix (deterministic), so 'noisy' here means biased-but-deterministic, not stochastic. Route 4 (statistical gating) is explicitly out of scope for this task, and shot noise is the mechanism that would make it necessary.
  8. F8/M5 policy: on exhausting max_retries or reaching delta_tau_min, the trial step is committed to the trajectory even though the gate still rejects it -- there is nothing better to do at the minimum step size. log['accepted'] records this (False), so it stays usable as a quality signal; a run report should read 'forced through unaccepted' counts, not just 'steps needed a retry'.
